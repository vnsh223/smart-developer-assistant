Company Leave Policy

Employees receive 24 paid leave days per year.

Employees can take a maximum of 2 consecutive leave days without manager approval.

For leave longer than 2 consecutive days, manager approval is required.

Sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as possible.

Unused paid leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.
